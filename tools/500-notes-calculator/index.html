How to calculate ₹500 notes for any amount

The formula is straightforward: number of ₹500 notes = amount ÷ 500. If the amount is an exact multiple of 500 the division is clean; otherwise you take the whole-number part and cover the remainder with smaller denominations. For example, ₹1,20,000 ÷ 500 = 240 notes exactly, while ₹1,20,300 gives 240 notes plus a ₹300 remainder (one ₹200 note and one ₹100 note).

Because the ₹500 note is the highest-value banknote in active circulation in India since the ₹2,000 note was withdrawn by the Reserve Bank of India in May 2023, almost every large cash transaction — salary disbursement, property token payments, wedding expenses, or daily shop takings — is counted primarily in ₹500 notes. Knowing the count quickly helps you fill a denomination sheet, cross-check a bank deposit, or reconcile a till.

Tips for counting ₹500 notes accurately

  1. Sort all ₹500 notes into one pile, facing the same way, before counting.
  2. Count in groups of 10 or 20 and keep a running tally; this makes it easy to spot where a miscount happened.
  3. Use the Cash verification section above to compare the counted total with the target.
  4. For mixed cash, use the full Cash Denomination Calculator which covers every note and coin from ₹500 down to ₹1.
  5. Check notes for security features as you count, especially on high-value ₹500 notes.
